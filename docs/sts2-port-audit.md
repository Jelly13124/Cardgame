# Historical StS2 Port Audit — Superseded

**Status:** historical record only; superseded on 2026-07-20.

The project previously used a direct card-by-card StS2 Ironclad port as a
prototype scaffold. That model is no longer the source of truth. Several ported
cards were removed, Bleed and Heat were deleted, and Cowboy Bill now uses the
original Short Circuit / Overload / Loaded / critical-hit kit documented in:

- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-20-cowboy-bill-card-system-redesign.md`
- `battle_scene/card_info/player/*.json`
- `run_system/core/meta_progress.gd` (`DEMO_REWARD_POOLS`)
- `docs/catalog_html/index.html` (generated; do not hand-edit)

References to StS2 may still describe interaction rhythm or layout hierarchy.
They do not authorize copying rendered assets, fonts, ornamental chrome, card
art, or exact card designs. `docs/project-rules.md` and
`docs/art-style-reference.md` remain the visual source of truth.

For current content counts and mechanics, trust validated JSON and the generated
catalog rather than old design tables or commit-era card names.
